# Privacy

I use two analytics tools on this site:

- **GoatCounter** — counts visits for everyone, without cookies.
- **Google Analytics 4** — shows in more detail how pages are read. It runs only if you say yes in the banner. If you decline or don't choose, Google Analytics doesn't load, and nothing is sent to Google.

## GoatCounter {#goatcounter-analytics}

GoatCounter records which page you opened, the site that linked here, your browser and screen size, and your country. It also counts which button you click in the banner, with no link to you. It doesn't set cookies or store your IP address, and it can't recognize you on a later visit. For details, see the [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

The choice is saved in your browser's local storage, so it applies to this browser only. If you turn analytics off, the site also deletes the Google Analytics cookies.

## What Google Analytics collects

If you accept, Google Analytics records:

- **Pages** — which pages you open, how long you stay, and how far you scroll.
- **Source** — the site or link that brought you here, such as LinkedIn or a search engine.
- **Device and location** — your browser, device type, and approximate city and country.

It doesn't collect your name, email, or IP address, and I don't use it for ads. Google Analytics stores the data for 14 months. To learn how Google handles it, see [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
